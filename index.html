The Goobers are a team within the competitive division. This team is externally competitive, not internally competitive. This means that The Goobers are looking to avoid playing with teammates who prevent them from getting in or won't pass them the ball or shame them for making mistakes. This season, we're formalizing The Goobers as a team explicitly committed to fairly distributed playing time, and a non-competitive spirit that prioritizes fun over winning.
