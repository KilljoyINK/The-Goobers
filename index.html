ROOM TO MESS UP.

The Goobers are a team within the competitive division that are externally competitive, not internally competitive. This means that The Goobers are looking to avoid playing with teammates who prevent them from getting in or won't pass them the ball or shame them for making mistakes. The Goobers as a team are explicitly committed to fairly distributed playing time, and a non-competitive spirit that prioritizes fun over winning.
